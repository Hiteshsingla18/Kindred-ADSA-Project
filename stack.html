HOW STACK WORKS

Like a pile of route cards.

PUSH puts a new route on top. POP removes only the top route. That is why a stack is called LIFO: Last In, First Out.

push A→push B→pop B

Route history stack

The orange card is always the top. Undo removes that card first.

Complexity

Only the top is changed. The bottom routes stay safe until they reach the top.
